openvireo-core-jvm/com.openvireo.core

Package-level declarations

The binding itself, generated by UniFFI from the ov-ffi crate: OvClient, Account, Vault, ServiceAccount, SelfHosted, the records and enums they take and return, the Host interfaces (HttpTransport, SecureStore, BlobCache, Clock, LogSink), OvException and the free functions (crypto, share links, TOTP, OPAQUE). Network and disk calls are suspend functions; from Java, use the wrappers in com.openvireo.core.java.
